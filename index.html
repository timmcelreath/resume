Room 1

Restorations

Four works, January–October 2026

Each work in this room takes something old and makes it usable again: the first films, a repertory film catalog, a parlour game and the first text adventure. Three are shown as they look today. The fourth still works: type into the terminal.

TM.2026.01

Criteria

Python (FastAPI), Next.js, React Native, MCP
Begun January 2026

A companion to the Criterion Channel. It adds full credits to every film, follows a person through their filmography, and keeps track of how much of each programme is still streaming.

TM.2026.03

Colossal Cave

JavaScript and three.js, after Fortran IV (1977)
Begun June 2026

A faithful port of Will Crowther's original 1977 text adventure, from before Don Woods expanded it. The engine follows the Fortran source line by line, and a second version draws the cave around you in first person. The terminal on this plate knows the first few rooms, word for word.

TM.2026.04

Old Lady Mahjong

React, Express, Supabase
Begun September 2026

Online mahjong in four styles: Hong Kong Old Style, Japanese Riichi, American and Sichuan. The server enforces every rule. You can play AI opponents or invite friends, and Auntie's lessons teach newcomers. Her opening line: “Sit down. I already shuffled.”
